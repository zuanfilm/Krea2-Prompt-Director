# Domain Model

VizClick does not model humans.

VizClick models creative subjects.

A subject may be:

- Human
- Animal
- Robot
- Mythological creature
- Alien
- Vehicle
- Abstract entity
- Environment
- Any fictional or real creative subject

The ontology describes semantic behavior rather than biological characteristics.

Likewise, creative intent does not necessarily originate from a human.

```
Human ─┐
        │
AI ─────┤
        ▼
Creative Representation Layer
        ▼
Creative Ontology
        ▼
Activation Network
        ▼
Production Brief
```

The first participant in the creative process may be either a human or an artificial intelligence.

The ontology only represents the resulting creative state.

It does not encode who originated it.

This distinction allows VizClick to support collaborative workflows where humans and AI continuously contribute to the same creative representation.
