# Actors

Actors describe every intentional element that occupies a role within a
Creative Representation.

An Actor is defined by the creative role it performs rather than its
real-world classification.

Actors may represent people, products, vehicles, architecture, animals,
objects, environments, or fictional creations.

Actors are renderer-independent and provide the foundation upon which
creative meaning is built.

## Purpose

The Actors domain defines reusable concepts that describe what exists
within a creative work.

Actors become expressive through their Properties, Appearance,
Performance, Pose, Motion, and Relationships.

## Examples

- Human
- Animal
- Vehicle
- Product
- Building
- Landscape
