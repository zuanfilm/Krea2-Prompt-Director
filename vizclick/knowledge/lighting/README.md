# Lighting

> **The creative domain that describes how illumination shapes the visual perception, atmosphere, emotion, and spatial understanding of a scene.**

Lighting is one of the core domains of the **Creative Knowledge Ecology (CKE)**.

Rather than describing technical lighting setups or renderer-specific
parameters, Lighting represents reusable creative knowledge about the
visual effects of illumination.

Lighting concepts are renderer-independent.

They describe **creative intent** rather than implementation.

---

# Purpose

The Lighting domain provides reusable Creative Concepts that describe how
light contributes to visual storytelling.

Lighting influences:

- Mood
- Atmosphere
- Emotional perception
- Subject emphasis
- Depth perception
- Spatial separation
- Time of day
- Visual hierarchy
- Texture perception
- Narrative focus

These concepts become building blocks for Creative Briefs and may be
combined with concepts from other domains through semantic reasoning.

---

# Design Principles

## Renderer Independent

Lighting concepts never describe renderer syntax, software settings, or
technical implementation.

Examples of excluded information include:

- exposure values
- lumen intensity
- rendering parameters
- camera settings
- engine-specific keywords

Those belong to Renderer Adapters.

---

## Creative Representation

Lighting concepts represent observable visual outcomes.

For example:

- Soft Diffused Lighting
- Golden Hour
- Rim Lighting

rather than instructions such as:

- increase exposure
- use area light
- enable volumetrics

---

## Reusable Knowledge

Each Lighting concept is intended to be reusable across:

- photography
- cinema
- illustration
- concept art
- animation
- game cinematics
- future rendering technologies

---

## Semantic Relationships

Lighting concepts participate in semantic relationships throughout the
Creative Knowledge Ecology.

For example:

Golden Hour

may be associated with:

- Warm Atmosphere
- Long Shadows
- Golden Glow
- Sunset Colors
- Nostalgia

These relationships enable probabilistic reasoning within the Visual
Meaning Layer (VML).

---

# Role within VizClick

Lighting concepts are selected by creators as part of the creative
process.

The Visual Meaning Layer (VML) may activate related Lighting concepts
through semantic reasoning.

Creators remain free to:

- accept recommendations
- ignore recommendations
- extend them through Custom Creative Intent (CCI)

The final Lighting Brief represents both semantic understanding and
human creative direction.
