# Dynamics

> **The creative domain that describes the perceived energy, movement, rhythm, and visual forces within a Creative Representation.**

Dynamics is one of the core domains of the **Creative Knowledge Ecology (CKE)**.

Rather than describing physical simulation or animation systems,
Dynamics represents reusable creative knowledge about how energy and
movement are perceived within a scene.

Dynamics extends beyond physical motion.

It encompasses the visual forces that create a sense of action,
stillness, rhythm, momentum, tension, or balance.

A Creative Representation may feel highly dynamic even when every Actor
remains physically static.

Dynamics concepts are renderer-independent.

They describe **creative behavior** rather than technical implementation.

---

# Purpose

The Dynamics domain provides reusable Creative Concepts that describe how
energy flows through a Creative Representation.

Dynamics influences:

- Movement
- Rhythm
- Pace
- Energy
- Momentum
- Stability
- Balance
- Tension
- Chaos
- Flow
- Impact
- Visual force

These concepts become building blocks for Creative Briefs and may be
combined with concepts from other domains through semantic reasoning.

---

# Design Principles

## Renderer Independent

Dynamics concepts never describe renderer syntax, animation systems, or
simulation parameters.

Examples of excluded information include:

- animation curves
- physics engines
- particle systems
- frame interpolation
- simulation settings

These belong exclusively to Renderer Adapters.

---

## Creative Representation

Dynamics concepts represent the perceived behavior of a scene rather than
its physical implementation.

For example:

- Graceful
- Explosive
- Flowing
- Chaotic

rather than:

- apply rigid body simulation
- increase velocity
- enable motion blur

---

## Reusable Knowledge

Dynamics concepts are reusable across:

- photography
- cinema
- animation
- illustration
- concept art
- game cinematics
- virtual production
- future rendering technologies

---

## Semantic Relationships

Dynamics concepts participate in semantic relationships throughout the
Creative Knowledge Ecology.

For example:

Explosion

may be associated with:

- Debris
- Shockwave
- Fire
- Smoke
- Chaos
- Impact

Likewise,

Graceful

may be associated with:

- Elegance
- Balance
- Flow
- Calm
- Precision

These relationships enable probabilistic reasoning within the Visual
Meaning Layer (VML).

# Role within VizClick

Dynamics concepts are selected by creators to describe how a scene feels
rather than merely how it moves.

The Visual Meaning Layer (VML) may activate related Dynamics concepts
through semantic reasoning.

Creators remain free to:

- accept recommendations
- ignore recommendations
- extend them through Custom Creative Intent (CCI)

The final Dynamics Brief represents both semantic understanding and
human creative direction.

---

# Relationship to Performance

Dynamics and Performance complement one another while representing
different aspects of a Creative Representation.

Performance describes **how Actors behave**.

Dynamics describes **how energy behaves within the scene**.

For example:

A performer may remain perfectly still while the scene feels highly
dynamic through:

- dramatic lighting
- strong composition
- environmental forces
- camera movement
- visual rhythm

Likewise, an Actor may perform an energetic action within an otherwise
calm and stable environment.

Together, Performance and Dynamics describe both the behavior of the
participants and the behavior of the world they inhabit.

# Guiding Principle

> Dynamics represents the visual language of energy.
>
> It describes how a scene feels in motion, whether anything physically
> moves or not.
