# Dynamics

Dynamics describes the perceived energy, movement, tension, and flow within a Creative Representation.

Dynamics extends beyond physical motion to encompass the visual forces that create a sense of action, stillness, rhythm, or momentum.

A creative work may feel highly dynamic even when every Actor remains physically static.

Dynamics is renderer-independent and focuses on expressive behavior rather than physical simulation.

## Purpose

The Dynamics domain defines reusable concepts that influence the perceived energy and temporal qualities of a creative work.

Dynamics may emerge from Actors, camera language, environmental forces, composition, or the interaction between multiple creative elements.

## Examples

- Static
- Walking
- Running
- Flowing
- Floating
- Explosive
- Chaotic
- Graceful
- Tense
- Calm
